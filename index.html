    How to read this

    • Radar score (0–100): size, momentum, net TAO flow, emissions vs. market-cap share, pool depth, trading activity and GitHub development, each ranked against all subnets.
    • Value score (reports): only the undervaluation signals, averaged over the week or month. Subnets with steady selling or collapsing development are excluded from the top 10.
    • Value gap: share of network emissions divided by share of market cap. Above 1× the network pays more than the market values.
    • Dev gap: development rank minus size rank. Positive means it builds more than its size suggests.

    Data from the Bittensor chain and public GitHub repos, collected by a private radar. A research filter, not investment advice.
