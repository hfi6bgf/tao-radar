    How to read this

    • Investing score (long term, 0–100): researched fundamentals 55% (live paid product, revenue, buybacks, price vs web2 rivals, rated 0–10) plus on-chain value 45% (emissions vs market-cap share, dev gap, net TAO inflow, discount, depth over 30 days). Only researched subnets get one; ⚠ marks steady selling or collapsing development.
    • Trading score (short term, 0–100): 24h net TAO flow, 24h momentum, trading activity, depth and development, ranked against all subnets, refreshed hourly.
    • Revenue and buyback figures are mostly reported by the teams themselves.
    • Value gap: share of network emissions divided by share of market cap. Above 1× the network pays more than the market values.
    • Dev gap: development rank minus size rank. Positive means it builds more than its size suggests.

    Data from the Bittensor chain and public GitHub repos, collected by a private radar. A research filter, not investment advice.
